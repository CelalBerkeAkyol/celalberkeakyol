### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am deeply passionate about demystifying the complexities of financial markets through data. My primary focus is applying advanced Data Science and Deep Learning techniques to the intricate challenge of **company valuation** on the stock exchange.

I share my research, models, and findings on my platform, **[cassandra.com.tr](https://cassandra.com.tr)**, with the goal of fostering a data-first approach to financial analysis.

- 🔭 I’m currently developing and testing various models for **valuing companies on Borsa Istanbul**, aiming to provide objective, data-driven investment insights.
- 🚀 I publish my findings, tutorials, and open-source tools on **cassandra.com.tr** to contribute to a more transparent financial community.
- 🤝 I'm actively looking to collaborate with **researchers, data scientists, and finance enthusiasts** who want to contribute articles and analysis to my platform. If you're passionate about this field, let's build a knowledge hub together!
